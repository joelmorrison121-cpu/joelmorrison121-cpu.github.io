I’m a Computer Science student at Dublin City University, exploring web engineering and practical AI tooling — the kind of software people actually open twice.

Outside lectures I spend time with Redbrick, DCU’s networking society, picking up the unglamorous skills that make systems reliable: collaboration, debugging under pressure, and shipping something that works.

Lately I’m into clean interfaces, small well-made tools, and the overlap between product sense and code. I like projects that start as a question and end as something a friend can use.
